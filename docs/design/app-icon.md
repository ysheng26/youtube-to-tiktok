# Parts app icon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG for the iOS AppIcon asset. The icon shows a stack of lime video cards on charcoal, matching the app's accent color. iOS applies the rounded outer mask.

Asset: `Parts/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS app icon for Parts, an app that watches long videos as swipeable one-minute parts. Generate one square 1024x1024 icon artwork, not a mockup. Full-bleed opaque near-black charcoal background. A bold, distinctive lime-green sculptural mark: three subtly stacked rounded video cards, the front card with a simple dark play triangle cutout, suggesting a sequence of video parts. Use the app's existing acid-lime accent (#BAFA5C) against charcoal. Confident minimal geometric composition, large central silhouette, slight dimensional depth and restrained highlights, crisp edges, beautiful at small size. No words, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no devices, no surrounding border. Keep all background corners square; iOS applies its own rounded mask. Mark occupies about 65 percent of canvas, generously padded.
